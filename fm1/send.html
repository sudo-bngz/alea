alea → FM-1

Sends a bank of patches written in alea to an M-VAVE FM-1 running Felucca with the ALEA engine, over USB. Compile one with alea bass.alea -n 8 --fm1 bass.syx. Chrome or Edge; the page asks to use MIDI SysEx.
