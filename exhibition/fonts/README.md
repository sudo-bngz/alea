# Fonts for the exhibition

`noto-sans-jp-regular.ttf` and `noto-sans-jp-black.ttf` are Noto Sans JP
(Regular and Black), by Adobe and Google, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(`OFL.txt`). They are cut down to what the works need: Latin, kana, Japanese
punctuation and the kanji `kikai.alea` uses (its Reel too) (about 280 kB each instead of
5.7 MB). For other kanji, use the full font from
<https://fonts.google.com/noto/specimen/Noto+Sans+JP> in their place.
