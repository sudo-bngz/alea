# Brut

Inputs of `../brut.alea`, made from a phone clip:

    python3 make-inputs.py IMG_4094.mov     # → sheets/hi0–6.jpg, sheets/lo.jpg, chop.wav
    python3 gen.py ../brut.alea             # the work itself

`globe.png` and `word.png` are the label's logo (its sphere and its
wordmark). The sheets and `chop.wav` are not in the repository.
